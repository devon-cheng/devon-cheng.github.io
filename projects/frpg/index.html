01 / IDEA

A workout log with RPG progression.

I wanted training to feel connected across sessions. I designed and built the SwiftUI app, combining guided workouts and fitness charts with quests, character growth, and inventory.

02 / DECISIONS

Base progression on logged workouts.

Keep logging practical

Sets, reps, weight, and rest stay together. Saved drafts recover interrupted workouts.

Use one workout record

The same performed sets feed fitness charts, XP, attributes, and quests. Rewards update when workout history changes.

See the campaign and inventory
